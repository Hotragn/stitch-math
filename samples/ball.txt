Little Ball (amigurumi)
Worked in continuous rounds with US terms. Stuff as you go.
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) x 6 (18)
Rnd 4: (sc 2, inc) x 6 (24)
Rnd 5: sc in next st, inc, (sc 3, inc) x 5, sc 2 (30)
Rnds 6-9: sc around (30)
Rnd 10: (sc 3, dec) x 6 (25)
Rnd 11: (sc 2, dec) x 6 (18)
Rnd 12: (sc, dec) x 6 (12)
Rnd 13: dec x 6 (6)
Fasten off and close the hole.
