Eyelet Scarf
One size. Worsted yarn, US 8 needles.
Cast on 30 sts.
Rows 1-4: Knit.
Row 5 (RS): K3, *yo, k2tog; rep from * to last 3 sts, k3.
Row 6: K3, purl to last 3 sts, k3.
Row 7: K3, *k2tog, yo; rep from * to last 3 sts, k3. (30 sts)
Row 8: K3, purl to last 3 sts, k3.
Repeat Rows 5-8 until scarf measures 60 in.
Rows 1-4: Knit.
Bind off all sts.
