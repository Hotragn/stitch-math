## Prompt
Build something with open-weight models or open-source AI that someone would actually pay for. The open pieces must be what makes the project work. Think small, specific and useful: a freelancer, a small business owner, a niche hobby community.

The post must explain why open innovation matters: cost, privacy, a fine-tune for a niche, avoiding lock-in.

Bonus: a first user. Did they pay? Would they? What did they ask for next?

## Judging
1. Writing Quality (weighted most): clear and engaging; what you built, who it's for, why open matters.
2. Relevance: open AI at the core, and someone would actually pay for it.
3. Creativity: original, or a fresh take on a familiar problem.
4. Technical Execution: does it work, and is it well built?
5. Partner tech (optional): meaningful use.

Ties go to the entry with the most positive reactions.

## FAQ rules that bind us
- New project only, started and finished inside the window (Oct 12 to 18). PRs to existing repos don't count.
- **Commits after the deadline must be noted in the README, or we may be disqualified.**
- One submission per challenge. It can win once, and it's considered for overall plus every partner category we list.
- Building on open source is fine if our changes are significant; credit prior work.
- English only for prizes.
- AI use is allowed.
- Partner categories need the tech to be used, and listed under Prize Categories.
