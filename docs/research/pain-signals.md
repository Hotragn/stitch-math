## Strongest paid-demand niches

1. **Personal-injury and disability legal support**: medical chronologies, record summaries, deposition summaries and scoping. People pay $5-$1,500 per file, $1-$1.50 per transcript page and $25-$85 per deposition summary. Rows 5-9. All of this data is private (medical and legal).
2. **Roofing and insurance-restoration contractors**: Xactimate estimates and supplements. People pay $100-$250 per estimate, with a $199 flat gig and a $100-per-estimate job post. Rows 10-12.
3. **UK landlords and letting agents**: inventory and check-out reports, needed at every tenancy. People pay £70-£216 per report, or about £5-£15 per property per month for software. Rows 16-17.
4. **Real estate photographers and agents**: HDR photo editing and floor-plan redraws for every listing. People pay $1-$5 per photo and $20-$65 per plan. Rows 49-50.
5. **Embroidery and apparel shops**: digitizing art into stitch files. People pay up to $50 per design, and top sellers have 5,000+ reviews each. Row 33. This is the hardest of the six to automate well.
6. **Bookkeepers**: bank-statement PDF to Excel/CSV conversion. People pay up to $50-$100 per job, or $30-$99/mo for SaaS, and one converter reached $12.5K MRR. Rows 1-4. This niche is already crowded: an HN search shows at least 7 different bank-statement converters launched between Nov 2025 and Aug 2026 (https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search?query=bank%20statement%20pdf%20csv&tags=story).

Close runners-up:
- HVAC Manual J reports: $65-$800 each (row 13).
- Vet and therapist note tools: $19-$400/mo (rows 46-48).
- Church sermon clips: $19-$997/mo (rows 52-53).
- Podcast show notes: up to $200 per episode, and Podsqueeze reached $6.5K MRR in 2 months (rows 21-22).
