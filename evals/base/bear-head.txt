Bear Head
Crochet, US terms, worked in continuous rounds. 3.5 mm hook.
Rnd 1: 6 sc in magic ring (6)
Rnd 2: inc in each st around (12)
Rnd 3: (sc, inc) x 6 (18)
Rnd 4: sc, inc, (sc 2, inc) x 5, sc 1 (24)
Rnd 5: (sc 3, inc) x 6 (30)
Rnd 6: sc 2, inc, (sc 4, inc) x 5, sc 2 (36)
Rnd 7: (sc 5, inc) x 6 (42)
Rnd 8: sc 3, inc, (sc 6, inc) x 5, sc 3 (48)
Rnds 9-16: sc around (48)
Rnd 17: sc 3, dec, (sc 6, dec) x 5, sc 3 (42)
Rnd 18: (sc 5, dec) x 6 (36)
Rnd 19: sc 2, dec, (sc 4, dec) x 5, sc 2 (30)
Rnd 20: (sc 3, dec) x 6 (24)
Stuff firmly.
Rnd 21: sc, dec, (sc 2, dec) x 5, sc 1 (18)
Rnd 22: (sc, dec) x 6 (12)
Rnd 23: dec x 6 (6)
Fasten off.
