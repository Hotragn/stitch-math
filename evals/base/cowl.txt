Lace Cowl
Sizes: Short (Long)
Cast on 120 (144) sts. Join in the round.
Rnds 1-6: *K1, p1; rep from * around.
Rnd 7: *Yo, k2tog, k4; rep from * around. 120 (144) sts.
Rnd 8: Knit.
Rnd 9: *K3, yo, k2tog, k1; rep from * around. 120 (144) sts.
Rnd 10: Knit.
Repeat Rnds 7-10 until cowl measures 10 (14) in.
Rnds 11-16: *K1, p1; rep from * around.
Bind off all sts loosely.
