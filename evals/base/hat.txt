Ridge Hat
Sizes: Child (Teen, Adult S, Adult L)
Yarn: worsted weight, 120 (150, 170, 200) yds. Needles: US 7 circular and DPNs.
Gauge: 20 sts and 28 rnds = 4 in in stockinette.

Cast on 64 (72, 80, 88) sts. Place marker and join to work in the round.
Rnds 1-10: *K2, p2; rep from * around.
Rnd 11: Knit.
Rnds 12-40 (12-44, 12-48, 12-52): Knit.

Crown
Rnd 1: *K6, k2tog; rep from * around. 56 (63, 70, 77) sts.
Rnd 2: Knit.
Rnd 3: *K5, k2tog; rep from * around. 48 (54, 60, 66) sts.
Rnd 4: Knit.
Rnd 5: *K4, k2tog; rep from * around. 40 (45, 50, 55) sts.
Rnd 6: Knit.
Rnd 7: *K3, k2tog; rep from * around. 32 (36, 40, 44) sts.
Rnd 8: *K2, k2tog; rep from * around. 24 (27, 30, 33) sts.
Rnd 9: *K1, k2tog; rep from * around. 16 (18, 20, 22) sts.
Rnd 10: K2tog around. 8 (9, 10, 11) sts.
Cut yarn, thread through remaining sts and pull tight. Weave in ends.
