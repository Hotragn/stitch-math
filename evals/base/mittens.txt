Simple Mittens
Sizes: Child (Adult S, Adult L)
Cast on 32 (40, 44) sts and join in the round.
Rnds 1-12: *K2, p2; rep from * around.
Rnd 13: Knit, increasing 2 (0, 2) sts evenly. 34 (40, 46) sts.
Rnds 14-18: Knit.
Thumb gusset
Rnd 19: K1, m1, k to end. 35 (41, 47) sts.
Rnd 20: K2, m1, k to end. 36 (42, 48) sts.
Rnd 21: K3, m1, k to end. 37 (43, 49) sts.
Rnd 22: K4, m1, k to end. 38 (44, 50) sts.
Rnd 23: K5, place 5 sts on hold for thumb, k to end. 33 (39, 45) sts.
Rnds 24-40 (24-46, 24-50): Knit.
Top
Rnd 1: *K1, k2tog; rep from * to end. 22 (26, 30) sts.
Rnd 2: Knit.
Rnd 3: K2tog around. 11 (13, 15) sts.
Break yarn and draw through remaining sts.
